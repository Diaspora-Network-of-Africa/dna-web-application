## 6. The full picker: what Strand's part does not carry (Done Means 4, the stop point)

Both pickers were read. `src/components/dna/ReactionPicker.tsx` is Frimousse styled to Strand (56-41E):
its search, skin tone, Recent and groups read the app's emoji data through `src/lib/emoji.ts`'s
resolver from the app's own origin (1351, 1577). Strand's full view can be fed the same groups, names
and Recent from that module. As compiled it does not carry four things the app picker does today:

1. **Search over the library's data.** Strand narrows "to the emoji whose name contains it" (names
   only). Frimousse searches labels and emojibase's tags, so "happy" finds Grinning face. Carrying tags
   would be a divergence from the compile's search.
2. **Toned picks across the library.** Strand's `withTone` tones nine literal hand characters;
   Frimousse applies the chosen tone to every emoji with skins (a wave, a raised hand, a fist). The port
   takes `takesTone` per emoji from the data, which carries it, but that is the port's translation, not
   the compile's.
3. **A virtualised grid.** Frimousse renders rows as they scroll; Strand renders every cell of every
   group in the DOM, about 3,700 buttons at emojibase 17, inside a Sheet at 390. Unproven on a phone
   until read on the deployed preview; a stall on open is the risk (moderate confidence).
4. **Arrow-key movement across the grid.** Frimousse moves the active cell with the arrow keys;
   Strand's grid is plain buttons, Tab only.

The quick view is bound (section 3). The full view is ported, exported and bound by nothing.
**Ruling 1776** (Notion D1879, 14 October 2026): the Messenger keeps the Frimousse picker, its pointer
panel and its Sheet as its full picker; Strand's part stays ported whole, the quick view bound, the full
view exported and unbound; nothing is deleted. The two implementations and the four things above are
**G294**, which closes when a later compile carries all four and its port binds Strand's full view and
deletes the Frimousse picker in the same PR.
