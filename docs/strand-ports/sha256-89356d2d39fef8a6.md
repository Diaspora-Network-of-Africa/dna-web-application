### AppHeader and ConsoleShell, the header mark (1892)

The compile draws a `span` with `role="img"` and `aria-label="DNA"`, a background of `var(--ink)` and
`wordmark-mono.svg` as the mask at `center / contain no-repeat`, at the old heights (AppHeader 24
compact and 28 expanded, ConsoleShell 24).

**Divergence, one.** The compile sizes the span's width as the height times 3.2022, the wordmark's
ratio written as a number. The handoff (item 4, under 184) requires the width from the file, with no
hardcoded ratio. So the span holds an `img` of the same file at the mark's height, width auto,
`visibility: hidden`, `alt=""`: the image takes its width from the file's `viewBox`, the span takes
the image's box, and the mask paints `--ink` over exactly that box. The next wordmark lands as a file
overwrite whatever its ratio. The mask and the image are one URL.

The app's three other sites that drew `logo.png` as a mark, `AuthHead`, the public profile's header
in `ProfileSurface` and the public event's header in `PublicEventSurface` (all in
`src/components/dna/`), take the same mechanism at their own heights; the compile's UI kit headers
are not ported.
